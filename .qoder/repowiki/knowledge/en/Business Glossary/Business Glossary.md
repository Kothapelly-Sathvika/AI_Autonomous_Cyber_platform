### Threat Intelligence
- Definition：Database of known malicious indicators including IP addresses, domains, and countries used to automatically compare against new traffic and identify potential threats before they reach the system
- Aliases：threat_intel、malicious indicators、blacklist

### Incident Management
- Definition：Workflow for tracking, filtering, and resolving detected cyber threats with status progression from Open → Investigating → Resolved, including evidence logging and export capabilities
- Aliases：incident center、incident tracking、threat lifecycle

### Automated Response
- Definition：Automatic mitigation actions triggered when high/critical threats are detected, including IP blocking, evidence logging, incident creation, and notification dispatch without human intervention
- Aliases：auto-response、automated mitigation、automatic blocking

### Severity Classification
- Definition：Four-tier threat rating system (Low, Medium, High, Critical) calculated from AI confidence scores, network metrics, and contextual factors to determine response urgency and alarm triggering
- Aliases：severity levels、threat severity、risk scoring

### Attack Simulation
- Definition：Continuous generation of realistic cyber attack patterns (brute force, DDoS, malware, phishing, etc.) used to demonstrate platform capabilities and test detection algorithms during development and demonstration
- Aliases：attack simulation、synthetic attacks、threat simulation

### Confidence Score
- Definition：AI-generated probability metric (0-100%) indicating how certain the detection system is about an identified threat, derived from IsolationForest anomaly scoring and used in severity calculation
- Aliases：ai confidence、detection confidence、anomaly score

### Audit Log
- Definition：Immutable record of user actions within the system including logins, logouts, administrative changes, and security events for compliance and forensic analysis
- Aliases：audit trail、user activity log、security audit

### Threat Detection Accuracy
- Definition：Performance metric measuring the percentage of correctly identified threats versus false positives, displayed as a gauge chart in the analytics dashboard
- Aliases：detection accuracy、AI accuracy、false positive rate
